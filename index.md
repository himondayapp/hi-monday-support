Hi Monday is a multi-currency portfolio tracker for people who hold a bit of
everything in more than one place: stocks, ETFs, crypto, and cash, spread across
brokers and currencies. It puts all of it in one view.

If you need help, have feedback, or found a bug, we'd love to hear from you.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a transaction?
Tap **＋** and choose **Buy**, **Sell**, **Deposit**, or **Withdraw**. Then enter
the asset, amount, price, date, and any fees. Any transaction can also have tags
and a note.

### Does Hi Monday connect to my broker or bank?
No. You enter everything yourself, so you never give anyone a password, an API
key, or a read-only login. That's also why it works with any broker in any
country, and your trading accounts stay private.

### What can I track?
Stocks (US, Asia, Europe, and more), ETFs and index funds, cryptocurrencies,
cash in multiple currencies, and any other asset you log by symbol or name.

### How does currency conversion work?
Enter each trade in the currency it actually happened in. Hi Monday converts
your whole net worth into the one home currency you pick.

### How do I sign in?
With Apple or Google. Your data syncs between your Android and iOS devices, and
the app works offline too.

### How do I delete my account or data?
Email [pandyin@gmail.com](mailto:pandyin@gmail.com) and we'll remove your synced
data. Deleting the app from your device removes whatever it stored there.
