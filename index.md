## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a transaction?
Tap the **＋** button, choose **Buy**, **Sell**, **Deposit**, or **Withdraw**,
then enter the asset, amount, price, date, and any fees. You can add tags and
notes to any transaction.

### Does Hi Monday connect to my broker or bank?
No. Hi Monday uses **manual entry only** — you never share a password, API key,
or read-only credentials with anyone. This means it works with any broker in any
country, and your trading accounts stay private.

### What can I track?
Stocks (US, Asia, Europe, and more), ETFs and index funds, cryptocurrencies,
cash in multiple currencies, and any other asset you log by symbol or name.

### How does the currency conversion work?
Log each trade in the currency it actually happened in, and Hi Monday converts
your entire net worth to the one home currency you choose.

### How do I sign in?
Sign in with **Apple** or **Google**. Your data syncs across your Android and iOS
devices, and the app also works offline.

### How do I delete my account or data?
Email [pandyin@gmail.com](mailto:pandyin@gmail.com) and we'll remove your synced
data. Locally stored data is removed when you delete the app from your device.
